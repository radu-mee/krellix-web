# Krellix Pricing
Last updated: 2026-05-11

Canonical pricing page: https://krellixlabs.com/en/pricing

## Plans

### Free
- Price: $0.00 / month
- Unlimited chats
- 2 agents
- 2 projects
- 1 MB files per project
- 2 files per project
- Group agents chats

### Plus
- Price: $19.90 / month
- Unlimited chats
- 6 agents
- 4 projects
- 10 MB files per project
- 4 files per project
- Group agents chats

### Pro
- Price: $29.90 / month
- Unlimited chats
- Unlimited agents
- 10 projects
- 100 MB files per project
- 6 files per project
- Group agents chats

## Notes
- VAT may apply.
- Krellix reserves the right to adjust pricing.

## Billing FAQ summary
- You can cancel paid plans at any time.
- Refund requests are reviewed via support.
- No hidden fees are listed beyond subscription price.
